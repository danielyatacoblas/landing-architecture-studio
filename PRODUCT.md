# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js 16 + React 19 + TypeScript, Three.js (dynamic import) for the 3D sun study, GSAP + Lenis, static prerender on Vercel, node:test for solar math.

## Users

Primary: recruiters and clients reviewing Daniel Yataco's front-end work. In-fiction: Lima families and developers about to build, who cannot read plans but feel the sun.

## Product Purpose

Celosía is a fictional Barranco architecture studio. The page proves its method (design the shade first) with a working 3D sun study, shows projects and captures plot leads.

## Positioning

The only studio page where you move the Lima sun over a model and read the % of the patio in shade.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Real photography from Pexels. Projects, figures and addresses are demo data and labeled as such.
